# Trial Use Terms

## Scope

This project may be made available only for internal trial use with the author's permission.

## Restrictions

During any trial period, the receiving party may not:

- copy the system beyond approved trial access
- reverse engineer the system or its workflows
- resell, sublicense, or redistribute the system
- claim that the project was developed, funded, commissioned, or owned by the organization

## Ownership

Trial access does not transfer any ownership, control, or intellectual property rights.

All core assets remain with the author unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

## Access Control

The author may suspend, revoke, or end trial access at any time.

## No Implied Transfer

Use of exported data, screenshots, or a demo environment does not grant rights to source code, deployments, prompts, databases, scripts, or review logic.
